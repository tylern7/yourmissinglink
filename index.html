BioSignature Modulation

Fat-loss that’s individualized for your body

Developed by world-renowned strength coach Charles Poliquin, BioSignature Modulation is a cutting-edge, non-invasive fat-loss and health assessment method. Pain-free performance: training principles

Getting hurt and sitting on the sidelines shouldn’t be your result after working out. Most trainees don’t use proper execution. By learning what the full range of motion means, you’ll be on the proper pathway to the best exercises:

  • Proper pressing to save your shoulders, and exercises to keep them healthy
  • Real squats for maximum performance and knee stability
  • Optimal core training for the lower back, and more

Many principles are based on Eastern European training methods, as well as current findings in the scientific literature.

The surprising story about the founder

I was always told I couldn’t succeed at my health and fitness goals; my bone structure was too small and I wasn’t lean enough. I worked hard in the gym but never got the results I wanted. Frustrated with “mainstream myths”, I searched until I found common sense, proven principles that produce real results.

I’ve been the skinny kid, overweight, and motivated to learn how to shape a championship body, all natural and while working as a busy professional. I sacrificed a successful business career to pursue my passion of helping others achieve their health and fitness goals.

While growing up, being active was always part of my lifestyle. My first job was working with children as a sports camp counselor, and I co-founded a youth fitness program. Still, at age 20 I weighed only around 140 lbs.

I studied finance and accounting at the University of Waterloo, graduating with a BA and later a Master of Accounting before obtaining my Chartered Accountant (CA) designation. Through school I got up over 200 lbs, too much of it unwanted fat, despite eating what I thought was “clean”. More steady “cardio” didn’t help either. In 2005 I was 190 lbs and 21% body fat.

In 2008 I experienced a BioSignature assessment. I had a high stress profile and wasn’t very carbohydrate tolerant. Within weeks I lost over 10 pounds of fat without even trying a fat-loss protocol. That led to my BioSignature certification, and to the competitive stage.

In October 2008 I entered my first fitness modeling competition with Ultimate Fitness Events, placed 2nd and won best costume. I won my first show in 2009. The most important lesson was the value of hard work and a positive mindset: where the mind goes, the body follows.

I promised myself I would do my best to see that others don’t stay frustrated, and became a certified BioSignature practitioner and PICP Level 2 coach. Whatever your reason, we can work together to end your frustrations.
